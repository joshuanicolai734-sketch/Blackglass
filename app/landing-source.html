GOOD TO KNOW
What happens after I apply?

Josh reads your enquiry and contacts you to discuss your goal, start date, and whether coaching is a fit. You’ll see the written scope and terms before committing. No payment is taken through this form.

Do I need the app to start coaching?

No app download is needed to enquire. The Android app shown here is in development; Josh will confirm the tools used for coaching before you start.

What does the founding price cover?

NZ$59 each week for 12 weeks, NZ$708 in total. It includes an initial training plan, one check-in each week, and training adjustments. Josh will confirm the full service and payment terms in writing before you commit.

Personal programme scope and price are agreed separately. The app is in development and is not presented as part of the paid coaching offer.
