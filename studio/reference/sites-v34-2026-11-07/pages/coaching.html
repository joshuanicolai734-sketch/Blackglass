01Who it’s for

Built for real weeks.

For people who want to get stronger, build a physique they’re proud of, and stop guessing what comes next, while fitting training around work and everything else.

Josh lifts, has an interest in MMA, and trains around a full work week himself. Your plan is built the same way: around the days and time you actually have, because the work you can repeat is the work that changes you.

Questions05 answers

Good to know.

What happens after I enquire?

Josh reads your enquiry and contacts you to talk through your goal, start date and whether coaching is a good fit. You'll see the written scope and terms before committing. No payment is taken through this form.

What does the founding price cover?

NZ$59 each week for 12 weeks, NZ$708 in total. It includes an initial training plan built around your week, one check-in each week, and training adjustments as you progress. Josh confirms the full service and payment terms in writing before you commit.

Do I need to be in Dunedin?

No. Coaching runs in person in Dunedin, or online anywhere in New Zealand.

Do I need the app to start coaching?

No. The Android app is in development and isn't part of the paid coaching offer. Josh will confirm the tools used for coaching before you start.

Can I get a programme without weekly coaching?

Yes. Choose "Programme" in the enquiry form. Its scope and price are agreed separately.
