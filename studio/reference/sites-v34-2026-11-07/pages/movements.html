Squat / Barbell

Back squat

Follow the bar as the hips and knees bend, then watch the figure return to standing.

  1. 01 Set up

    The feet stay planted and the bar rests across the upper back.

  2. 02 Lower

    The hips and knees bend together as the bar moves down.

  3. 03 Stand

    The figure rises to the starting position with the bar on the upper back.

Hinge / Barbell

Deadlift

Watch the bar leave the floor, travel close to the body and settle back onto the ground.

  1. 01 Set up

    The bar starts on the floor, close to the shins.

  2. 02 Lift

    The legs and hips extend; the bar rises with the arms straight.

  3. 03 Lower

    The hips and knees bend as the bar returns to the floor.

Press / Bodyweight

Push-up

Compare the body line on the way down and on the way back up, from two angles.

  1. 01 Set up

    The hands and toes support a long body line.

  2. 02 Lower

    The elbows bend and the chest approaches the floor.

  3. 03 Press

    The arms extend and the body rises together.
