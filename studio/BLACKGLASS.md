# Blackglass

Blackglass is a training and coaching product with a distinctive, engineered visual identity. The experience should be useful, understandable and responsive on real phones, with visual ambition that serves the product.

## Current direction
- Preserve the approved bold light/dark direction and sleek synthetic-body concept where the task calls for it
- Keep the experience precise, physical and human-readable; technical styling must communicate something useful
- Preserve useful content, working flows and data while improving their presentation
- Do not substitute a generic dashboard, decorative science-fiction interface or older crimson-only brief for the accepted task direction

These are product intentions, not a claim that every feature or visual is implemented or available. Confirm the exact approved brief, source and release target for each task. Private preview assets must not be copied into this public repository.
